Hero description
Guide a bat through gaps between stone columns. Tap to climb, hold to slow your descent, and try to beat your best score.

Gameplay section
Control your flight
Tap for a wingbeat. Hold to spread your wings and descend 50% slower, then release to tuck them for a faster drop. Every column pair earns a point, and the challenge grows as you go.

World section
Inside TapBat
Fly past layered forests, stone columns, rocks, and ferns. Generate a different landscape in Settings, or keep the one you have.

Landscape section
Change the view
Choose New landscape in Settings to generate and save a different scene. Your personal best stays with you.
