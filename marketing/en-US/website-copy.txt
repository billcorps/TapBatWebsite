Hero description
A little bat. Moonlit ruins. One more point to beat your best. Fly between stone columns with wings that respond to every tap and hold.

Gameplay section
Find your rhythm
Tap for a wingbeat. Hold to spread your wings and descend 50% slower, then release to tuck them for a faster drop. Every column pair earns a point, and the challenge grows as you go.

World section
An overgrown world with depth
Forests pass in layers beneath drifting clouds. Vines wind around stone columns, and ferns grow among rocks on the slopes below. Procedural shapes and variation give the moonlit ruins their character.

Landscape section
Change the view
Choose New landscape in Settings to generate and save a different scene. Your personal best stays with you.
